## Function

- Description: Performs element-wise addition, multiplication, and division on multiple tensors. $x2_{i}$ and $x3_{i}$ are divided element-wise, the result is multiplied by scalar, and then added to $x1_{i}$.

- Formula:

  $$
  x1 = [{x1_0}, {x1_1}, ... {x1_{n-1}}], x2 = [{x2_0}, {x2_1}, ... {x2_{n-1}}], x3 = [{x3_0}, {x3_1}, ... {x3_{n-1}}]\\
  y = [{y_0}, {y_1}, ... {y_{n-1}}]\\
  $$

  $$
  y_i = {x1}_{i}+ \frac{{x2}_{i}}{{x3}_{i}}\times{scalar} (i=0,1,...n-1)
  $$
